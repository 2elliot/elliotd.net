Nocte

A game made for the Oregon Game Project Challenge 2023-2024 over the span of 9 months
Explore the world of Nocte, an open world 3d roguelike where you explore a completely procedural world. Collect weapons, fight enemies, and upgrade yourself.

HeavyVille

A game made for the Game Maker's Toolkit Game Jam 2024 over the span of 4 days
Value the items of the town's customers and make sure to keep them happy. In the town of Heavvyville, the price of an item is equal to its weight, so make sure to keep your scales accurate!

Symbiont

A game made for Oregon Game Jam Project 2022-2023 over the span of 3 months
Fight through an endless dungeon, teleporting between rooms and taking control of your enemies. But be careful, you have no control of your movement, so you must rely on your enemies to move you around the dungeon.
