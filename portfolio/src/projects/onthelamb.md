_In On The Lamb! you play as an engineer-turned-rancher in exile with an eldritch being in their backyard. Lasso sheep to add to your herd and use their wool to build wacky machines that unlock new horizons for all sheepkind—maybe even a chance to escape the living nightmare you've found yourself in._

We built this game as part of the GMTK 2025 game jam, over 4 days.
