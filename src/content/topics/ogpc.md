The Oregon Game Project Challenge (OGPC) is Oregon's statewide student game development competition. Unlike most game jams, OGPC runs over roughly nine months and is judged by a panel of industry professionals across design, art, and programming disciplines.

Teams submit a complete game alongside documentation, and compete for awards across multiple categories. The extended timeline allows for significantly more ambitious projects than a typical game jam — and places greater weight on iteration, scope management, and team communication.

## Fractal Development at OGPC

[Fractal Development](/topics/fractal-dev/) competed in OGPC for three consecutive years:

- [Symbiont](/projects/symbiont/) (2023) — Best in Programming
- [Nocte](/projects/nocte/) (2024) — Best Game Design
- [Northwoods](/projects/northwoods/) (2025) — Best in Show, Players' Choice
