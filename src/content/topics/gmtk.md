The GMTK Game Jam is an annual game development competition hosted by [Game Maker's Toolkit](https://www.youtube.com/@GMTK), a YouTube channel focused on game design analysis. Teams and individuals have just 96 hours to build a game around a surprise theme announced at the start of the jam.

It is one of the largest game jams in the world, regularly drawing tens of thousands of entries. Competitors may use pre-existing code or assets, but are not permitted to prepare content specifically for the competition ahead of time.

## Fractal Development at GMTK

[Fractal Development](/topics/fractal-dev/) has competed in the GMTK Game Jam twice:

- [Heavyville](/projects/heavyville/) (2024, theme: *Built to Scale*) — top 7% out of 7,500+ entries
- [On the Lamb!](/projects/sheep/) (2025, theme: *Loop*) — top 4% out of 9,500+ entries
- [Time to Impact](/projects/impact/) (2026, theme: *Countdown*) — top 2% out of 10,000+ entries
