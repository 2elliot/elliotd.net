Fractal Development is a student game development team based in Oregon. The team formed through a high school computer science program and has competed together for several years across state competitions and online game jams.

## Projects

Over three years, Fractal Development built five games across two formats: the nine-month [Oregon Game Project Challenge](/topics/ogpc/) and the four-day [GMTK Game Jam](/topics/gmtk/).

- [Symbiont](/projects/symbiont/) (2023) — OGPC entry. Won Best in Programming.
- [Nocte](/projects/nocte/) (2024) — OGPC entry. Won Best Game Design.
- [Heavyville](/projects/heavyville/) (August 2024) — GMTK Game Jam entry. Placed top 7% out of 7,500+ entries.
- [Northwoods](/projects/northwoods/) (2025) — OGPC entry. Won Best in Show and Players' Choice.
- [On the Lamb!](/projects/sheep/) (August 2025) — GMTK Game Jam entry. Placed top 4% out of 10,000+ entries.

## Team

Fractal Development has always been a small team of programmers. By the 2025 competition cycle, most of the original team members had graduated, making Northwoods and On the Lamb! the final projects made together as a full team.
